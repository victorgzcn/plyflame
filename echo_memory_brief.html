❓ Quick Answers

Q: Where is my data stored?

A: In your browser's localStorage. I never see it. You can export it anytime.

Q: Is it really free forever?

A: Yes. One HTML file. No servers. No costs. Free forever.

Q: Can I lose my data?

A: If you clear browser data, yes. Export regularly for backup.

Q: Why no AI?

A: I wanted something simple. AI costs money and needs servers. This is free and private.
